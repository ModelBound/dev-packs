<h1>Idea Refine</h1>
<h2>Overview</h2>
<p>Turn vague ideas into concrete, actionable proposals using structured divergent/convergent thinking. Don't jump to implementation — explore the problem space first.
</p>
<h2>When to Use</h2>
<ul><li>You have a rough concept that needs exploration</li>
<li>Multiple approaches seem viable and you need to choose</li>
<li>The problem isn't well-defined yet</li>
<li>Stakeholders have different ideas about what to build</li>
</ul>
<h2>The Process</h2>
<h3>Phase 1: Diverge — Explore the Space</h3>
<p>Generate multiple framings of the problem before settling on one:
</p>
<li><strong>Restate the idea in 3 different ways</strong> — each framing reveals different assumptions</li>
<li><strong>List what you know vs. what you're assuming</strong> — assumptions are the most dangerous part</li>
<li><strong>Generate at least 3 different approaches</strong> — don't stop at the first viable option</li>
<li><strong>Identify the riskiest assumption in each approach</strong> — what would invalidate this?</li>
<h3>Phase 2: Converge — Choose a Direction</h3>
<p>Evaluate options against concrete criteria:
</p>
<li><strong>Define success criteria</strong> — what does "done" look like?</li>
<li><strong>Score each approach</strong> against: feasibility, risk, time, reversibility</li>
<li><strong>Select the approach</strong> with the best tradeoff profile</li>
<li><strong>Document why you rejected the others</strong> — this prevents relitigating decisions</li>
<h3>Phase 3: Crystallize — Write the Proposal</h3>
<p>Produce a concrete proposal covering:
</p>
<ul><li>What we're building (not how)</li>
<li>Why this approach over alternatives</li>
<li>What success looks like</li>
<li>What we're explicitly NOT building</li>
<li>Open questions that need answers before proceeding</li>
</ul>
<h2>Anti-Patterns</h2>
<ul><li>Jumping to implementation before the problem is understood</li>
<li>Treating the first viable idea as the only idea</li>
<li>Skipping the "what are we NOT building" section</li>
<li>Leaving open questions unresolved</li>
</ul>
<h2>Verification</h2>
<ul><li>[ ] At least 3 approaches were considered</li>
<li>[ ] Assumptions are listed explicitly</li>
<li>[ ] Success criteria are specific and testable</li>
<li>[ ] Rejected approaches are documented with reasons</li>
<li>[ ] Open questions are listed</li>
</ul>
